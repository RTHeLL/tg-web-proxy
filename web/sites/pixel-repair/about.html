About the shop

Pixel Repair is a five-bench service shop on Congress Avenue. We opened in 2016 when the owners tired of franchise stores that quoted flat rates before opening a case.

Every device gets a written intake note: visible damage, reported symptoms, and test results from our bench power supplies. If a repair is not economical, we say so before ordering parts.

Bench standards

  • ESD-safe workstations and labeled parts bins
  • Quote approval required before non-trivial part orders
  • Recycling drop-off for batteries and unusable boards

We do not buy used devices on site and we do not unlock accounts. Bring your passcode or be ready to sign in during pickup testing.
