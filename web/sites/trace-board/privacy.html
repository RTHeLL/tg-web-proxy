Privacy

This marketing site is static HTML served without client-side analytics, social widgets, or third-party trackers. We do not collect form submissions on these pages.

Standard web server logs may record the date, requested URL, user agent, and IP address. Logs are retained for security monitoring and deleted on a rolling ninety-day schedule.

Customer telemetry processed by the Trace Board platform is governed by separate data processing agreements. Span payloads may contain service names, latency measurements, and optional attribute tags supplied by your instrumentation — never end-user credentials unless your team explicitly attaches them.

We do not sell visitor or customer data. Enterprise customers may request a data processing addendum covering EU and UK residency requirements.
