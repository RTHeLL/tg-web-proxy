Independent bookstore · Since 1998

A room for slow reading and unexpected finds.

Atlas Books is a single-floor shop on Mercer Street: fiction stacks, a poetry alcove, and a back table where the staff leaves what we finished last week.

Staff shelf

March picks include a river memoir, a debut story collection, and a reprint of a forgotten travelogue we cannot keep in stock.

Evening readings

Local authors on the last Thursday of each month. Chairs up front, tea in the back, no ticket required.

Special orders

Ask at the counter for titles we do not carry. Most arrive within five business days.

Open late on Fridays

We stay open until 21:00 for the neighborhood crowd that wanders in after dinner. The fiction room stays lit; the register closes at 20:45.
