Independent bookstore · Since 1998

A bookstore for people who read the last page slowly.

Fiction, poetry, essays, and regional history on a single floor in Ashford. Staff recommendations at the front table, chairs in the back, and readings on the last Thursday of every month.

Staff shelf — August

A river memoir set in the Connecticut Valley, a debut story collection from a Burlington writer, and a reprint of a 1962 travelogue that sells out within a week every time we restock it.

Evening readings

Local authors on the last Thursday of each month. Free admission. Tea in the back room; doors at 18:30, reading at 19:00.

Special orders

Ask at the counter. Most titles arrive within five business days. We call when they're in.

Used hardcovers

A rotating table of hardcovers from regular customers — literary fiction, nature writing, and the occasional out-of-print cookbook. Credit toward store purchases, no cash.

Children's corner

Picture books, middle-grade novels, and a small shelf of Vermont authors for young readers. Story time on Saturday mornings at 10:30.

Open late on Fridays

We stay open until 21:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. The fiction room stays lit; the register closes at 20:45 so the last customer has time to browse without hurry.
