About the shop

Atlas Books opened in 1998 when two librarians wanted a neighborhood bookstore in Ashford. We are still independent and still on Mercer Street.

Our inventory focuses on literary fiction, essays, poetry, and regional history. We have a small children's section and a table of used hardcovers from regular customers.

What we believe

  • Staff can recommend books and talk about what they have read
  • We keep chairs for browsing
  • We host local authors in person

The shop is worker-managed. We hold monthly meetings in the back office.
