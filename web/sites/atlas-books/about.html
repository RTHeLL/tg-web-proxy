About the shop

Atlas Books opened when two librarians decided Ashford needed a place to browse without a barcode scanner humming at every aisle. We are still independent, still on Mercer Street, and still allergic to algorithmic recommendations.

Our inventory leans literary fiction, essays, poetry, and regional history. We keep a small children's corner and a rotating table of used hardcovers donated by regulars.

What we believe

  • Staff should be able to talk about a book for more than ten seconds
  • Chairs belong in bookstores
  • Authors deserve a real audience, not a livestream overlay

The shop is worker-managed. Major decisions happen at monthly meetings over coffee in the back office.
