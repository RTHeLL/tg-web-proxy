Privacy

This QR menu is a static website with no order forms, payment fields, or analytics trackers. Browsing the menu does not create an account or store personal information on your device beyond normal browser caching.

Our hosting provider may log anonymous request metadata such as date, page URL, and IP address for security purposes. These logs are deleted after thirty days.

In-restaurant orders and payment details are handled at the point of sale by our staff. That information is not linked to your visit to this web menu.
