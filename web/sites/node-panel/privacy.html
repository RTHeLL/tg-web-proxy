Privacy

This website is a static brochure for Node Panel hosting services. We do not embed analytics pixels, social login widgets, or third-party scripts on these pages.

Web server logs may record timestamps, requested URLs, referrer headers, and IP addresses for security and capacity planning. Logs are rotated after thirty days unless required for an active abuse investigation.

Customer account data — billing details, SSH keys, and instance metadata — is stored separately in our control-plane database under GDPR-aligned retention policies. We do not sell account or visitor information.
