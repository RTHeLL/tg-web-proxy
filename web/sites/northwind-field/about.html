About the station

Northwind Field began as a shared shed and a spreadsheet. Four growers wanted consistent soil readings across neighboring quarter-sections without buying the same proprietary dashboard four times.

Today the station maintains plot trials, a small telemetry network, and a public archive of field notes. We are not a seed company, not a software vendor, and not a consultancy. We publish what we run on our own ground.

What we keep here

  • Cover-crop and rotation trial protocols with year-over-year results
  • Soil moisture, temperature, and rainfall logs from field sensor nodes
  • Equipment calibration notes for drills, spreaders, and moisture probes
  • Lab results for organic matter, infiltration, and aggregate stability

The archive is reference material. There is no newsletter, comment section, or account system on this site.
