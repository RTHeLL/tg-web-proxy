About the desk

Northwind Field Notes started as a binder on a pilothouse shelf. Over time it grew into a small archive for coastal survey work.

We publish tide corrections, compass logs, tool maintenance notes, and weather observations from working vessels.

This is a reference archive, not a store. There is no newsletter or comment section.

What we keep here

  • Annotated harbor charts and revision histories
  • Barometer and wind logs from working vessels
  • Notes on maintaining sextants, aneroids, and field compasses
