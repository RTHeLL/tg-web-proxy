About the desk

Northwind Field Notes began as a binder on a pilothouse shelf. What started as tide corrections and compass drift logs grew into a small studio archive for coastal survey writing.

We publish only what we would keep in that binder: short observations, tool care, and the kind of weather that changes a plan. Our work favors pencil marks on cotton paper over glossy dashboards.

There is no newsletter signup, no comment desk, and no marketplace. If you arrived looking for a product launch, you found an archive instead.

What we keep here

  • Annotated harbor charts and revision histories
  • Barometer and wind logs from working vessels
  • Notes on maintaining sextants, aneroids, and field compasses
