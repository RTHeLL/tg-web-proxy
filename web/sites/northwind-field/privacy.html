Privacy

This site serves static pages only. It does not run analytics scripts, does not open third-party embeds, and does not collect account details or payment information.

If you contact us by mail or telephone, we handle that correspondence outside this website. We do not link visitor log data to offline communications unless required for security or legal compliance.
