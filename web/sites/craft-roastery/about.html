About the roastery

Craft Roastery started in a warehouse bay with a used drum roaster. We still keep our early roast notes on a shelf near the cooling tray.

We buy through importers we know and rotate origins seasonally. Each lot gets at least three trial roasts before it goes on the menu.

Roasting philosophy

  • We watch development time, not just peak temperature
  • We rest beans before shipping
  • Grind recommendations are printed on every bag

The roastery floor is open for scheduled visits. Safety gear is required near active machinery.
