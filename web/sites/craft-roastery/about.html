About the roastery

Craft Roastery started in a borrowed warehouse bay with a used drum roaster and a notebook of failed batches. We still keep that notebook on a shelf near the cooling tray.

We buy through importers we have visited, favor transparent farm-gate pricing, and rotate origins instead of chasing novelty for its own sake. Every lot gets at least three trial roasts before it lands on the menu board.

Roasting philosophy

  • Development time matters more than peak temperature alone
  • Rest days are part of the recipe, not downtime
  • Grind recommendations ship with every bag

The roastery floor is open for scheduled visits. Safety gear is required near active machinery.
