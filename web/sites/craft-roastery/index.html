From green to cup

Every lot follows the same four-stage path through our warehouse roastery.

01

Sourcing

Importers send samples. We cup twice before committing to a lot size.

02

Profile

Three test roasts on the drum. Final curve logged and posted on the bag.

03

Roast day

Tuesday production on the twelve-kilo Probat. Rest and degas until Thursday.

04

Ship & cup

Orders out Wednesday. Open cupping table every Thursday at 17:30.
