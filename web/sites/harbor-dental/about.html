About Harbor Dental

Harbor Dental opened on Bayview Avenue in 2004 as a neighborhood practice focused on preventive care and clear communication. We treat children, adults, and seniors from Seaside and the surrounding coast.

Our treatment rooms use digital imaging and paperless charting. We explain every procedure in plain language and provide written estimates before restorative work is booked.

What to expect

  1. Front desk confirms insurance and arrival time by phone
  2. Hygiene or exam with imaging when indicated
  3. Verbal summary and written plan for any follow-up work
  4. Recall scheduling before you leave the office

Initial consultations for new patients are held during weekday mornings or Saturday slots. Evening appointments are reserved for returning patients and urgent restorative needs.
