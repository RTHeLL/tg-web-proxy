About our practice

Harbor Dental opened to give Seaside families a clinic that feels organized without feeling corporate. Dr. Naomi Reyes leads a small team of hygienists and coordinators who have worked together for years.

We invest in modern imaging and sterilization equipment, but we measure success by whether patients understand their options—not by how quickly we turn over a chair.

Our standards

  • Treatment plans in plain language, with printed summaries
  • Sedation alternatives discussed when anxiety is a barrier
  • Records transferred promptly when you change providers

The office is on the ground floor with accessible parking behind the building on Coral Lane.
