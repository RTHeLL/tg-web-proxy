About our practice

Harbor Dental opened in 2004 for Seaside families. Dr. Naomi Reyes leads a team of hygienists and front desk staff who have worked together for several years.

We use modern imaging and sterilization equipment. We explain treatment options before proceeding.

Our standards

  • Treatment plans in plain language, with printed summaries
  • Sedation alternatives discussed when anxiety is a barrier
  • Records transferred promptly when you change providers

The office is on the ground floor with accessible parking behind the building on Coral Lane.
