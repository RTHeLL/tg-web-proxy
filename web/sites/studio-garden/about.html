About the studio

Studio Garden is a landscape design practice focused on residential work in the Pacific Northwest. We draw by hand first, then refine in CAD when a contractor needs precise dimensions.

We use native and adapted plants and permeable surfaces where possible. We do not install hardscape ourselves. We work with local builders and check in during the first growing season.

How we work

  1. Site visit and light study
  2. Concept plan with material palette
  3. Construction documents and planting schedule
  4. Walk-through at completion and seasonal check-in

Initial consultations are held at our Linden Walk studio or on site within a thirty-mile radius.
